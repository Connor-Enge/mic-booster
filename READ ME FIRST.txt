===============================================================
  MIC BOOSTER  -  make your microphone louder (and better)
===============================================================

  Just run MicBooster.exe. Nothing to install.

  Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" box the first
  time, because the app isn't code-signed. Click "More info"
  then "Run anyway".


---------------------------------------------------------------
  IF YOU JUST WANT IT LOUDER (30 seconds, no setup)
---------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Pick your microphone in the "Microphone" box, top left.
     Talk. The meter should move. If it doesn't, wrong mic.

  2. Drag "Windows level" up.

  3. If "Hardware boost" isn't greyed out, set it to +10 or +20.

  That's it. Every app on your PC is now louder, immediately.
  You don't even need to press Start.


---------------------------------------------------------------
  IF YOU WANT IT TO SOUND GOOD TOO
---------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Set "Output device" (bottom) to your headphones.
  2. Pick the preset "Quiet Mic Rescue".
  3. Press START.

  You'll now hear yourself. Drag BOOST until the lower meter
  sits mostly in the green and amber. Red constantly = too much.

  Try the other presets. "Voice Chat" is the everyday one.


---------------------------------------------------------------
  TO MAKE IT SHOW UP AS A MIC IN DISCORD / ZOOM / OBS
---------------------------------------------------------------

  Look at the "Virtual microphone" panel at the bottom.

  Windows only lets a *driver* create a microphone, so no
  program can just invent one. Instead we send the processed
  sound down a free "virtual cable", and Discord listens to the
  other end of it. The app sets this up for you.

  FIRST TIME:

  1. If the panel says no cable was found, press
     "Get VB-CABLE (free)". Install it, then RESTART your PC.

  2. Press "Set up" in that panel. One click - it picks the
     right output and starts processing.

  3. Optional but nice: type a name in that panel and press
     "Rename". The device then shows up in Discord as
     "Mic Booster" instead of "CABLE Output (VB-Audio...)".
     Restart Discord afterwards so it notices the new name.
     "Restore" puts the old name back.

  4. In Discord: Settings > Voice & Video > INPUT DEVICE,
     choose that device.

  5. IMPORTANT: in the same Discord screen, turn OFF
     "Automatic Gain Control". If you leave it on, Discord
     re-adjusts your volume and undoes all the processing.
     This is the #1 reason people think it isn't working.

  Keep Mic Booster running with START pressed.

  Heads up: you can send to the cable OR hear yourself in your
  headphones, not both at the same time. Doing both needs a
  mixer app called VoiceMeeter. That's a Windows limitation.

  Don't want to bother with any of this? The "JUST WANT IT
  LOUDER" steps at the top work everywhere with no setup.


---------------------------------------------------------------
  SOMETHING'S WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------

  Meter doesn't move
      Wrong microphone selected, or Windows is blocking it:
      Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone >
      "Let desktop apps access your microphone" = On

  Discord is still quiet
      Either Discord is still set to your raw microphone
      instead of the cable, or Discord's "Automatic Gain
      Control" is still on. Check both.

  The new name didn't appear in Discord
      Restart Discord. Programs only read device names
      when they start up.

  Crackling / stuttering
      Raise "Buffer" (bottom) to 50 or 100 ms.

  Sounds distorted or crunchy
      BOOST is too high. Turn it down.

  It cuts off the ends of my words
      Turn the Gate off, or lower its Threshold.

  Sounds like it's breathing / pumping
      Raise Auto Level "Speed", or lower Compressor "Ratio".
